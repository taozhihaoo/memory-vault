# Sourdough Baking Journal

A sourdough starter is a living culture of wild yeast and lactic acid
bacteria. Feeding it daily with flour and water keeps the culture strong.

## Notes

- Hydration at 75% gives an open crumb.
- Bulk ferment takes about four hours at room temperature.
- Bake in a Dutch oven at 250°C for 20 minutes, then 230°C uncovered.
